# A001 Evidence Register

## Purpose

This document tracks every factual claim that must be verified before article publication.

## Required Evidence Categories

- Device compatibility
- Carrier unlock requirements
- eSIM hotspot support
- Fair-use policies
- Pocket WiFi rental terms
- Loss / damage policy
- Coverage statements
- Provider products
- Official pricing
- Affiliate destinations
- Internal links
- Publication date verification
